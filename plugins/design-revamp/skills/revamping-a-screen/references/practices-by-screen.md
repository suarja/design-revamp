# Practices by screen type

Mined from the shared design bank on 6 November 2026 (372 records, 38 before/after pairs). Each practice names a mechanism and the failure it avoids. Ids in parentheses resolve in the shared library described by design-bank, on the owner's Mac only: open them to see the mechanism at work, never copy, commit or publish the images. Elsewhere, search by the motif words instead.

A practice is a question to ask of your screen, not a look to apply. Apply it when it serves the sentence of the screen. The sources are other people's products and some carry visible flaws, so check a claim against the image before repeating it.

## What a revamp removes

The signature of a vibe-coded screen, as the before/after pairs show it.

- A box around everything. Remove the card and let the images make the structure; overflow at the edge says there is more (693fa340).
- Colour with no meaning: six coloured icons become four calm tiles (6a365de1); the pile loses its colours and gains a count (28539e2f).
- Metrics too small to read: five tiny measures become four legible ones, and the score takes half the attention (fafa26ba).
- A slogan where a position should be: « week 7 of 14 » says where you are (89043b47). A home answers "where am I today" (aa04aea3); a balance becomes "what is left and for how long" (f282002f).
- Competing entries: three become one prepared task (2d759065); four buy buttons become one recommendation (8bd04f97).
- Decoration standing in for the subject: the subject leaves the diagram and becomes the world of the screen (b15ae3da); figures sit in the place they describe (4223ed1e). A character with nothing to do leaves (aa04aea3).
- Long text in the list: fold it behind a chevron and the list is a list again (e8d79a60).

A revamp can also add: a super-app reads by its density, so removing would be wrong (d102a03c). Ask what the screen needs, not how to make it emptier.

## Paywall

- **Sell what the person gets, not a feature list.** Their own plan, computed and locked (3d7690ee). An object obtained per benefit (eaf0e780). The same photo in three states (10ec81c3). Gauges showing what this price buys and what the next tier adds (facc4cb9).
- **Open from the limit just reached.** The wall names what stopped them and what they lose nothing of. State what stays free in a full sentence.
- **Recommend one offer, and say for which situation.** The rest shrinks to a line. A sample result comes after the choice, as proof (8bd04f97).
- **The button names the offer and its price** (2be49b39), or what you obtain (0c4df672). Check the price on the button matches the offer selected: two references in the bank get this wrong (03023ffe, eaf0e780).
- **Show the unlock in place.** A blurred preview with its cost visible works when the blurred thing truly exists; never blur something that was not computed.
- **Stay in the world of the lesson.** A paywall that keeps the type and light of the screen before it is not a change of place (2be49b39).
- **Refuse politely.** A full-sentence refusal that says what happens next, without guilt (48949dbc, 03023ffe).
- **An exact amount decides where a percentage attracts**: « $240 » after « 80% » (9fef8d4c). An offer after refusal changes colour and shows one number, so it reads as a different screen (e6b66d04).
- **A heavy subject gets a proud character**: the character removes the guilt (7a0c5a0d).
- **Keep the store's legal line on every variant**: title, length, price, auto-renewal, restore. Legible, not hidden.

## Entry to a paid feature

A full component in the flow of the screen, not a small link or badge: illustration or character, a hook, a preview of the value computed for this reader, a full-width button, the price line (f5ff2732 places it at the top of a settings page, as a real card). Tiny chips are not understood.

## Profile

- **Make visible what the product believes it knows, say where it learned it, and let the person correct it.** Trust in a memory comes from that reading (28cec724). This is the page for any product with a memory.
- **Build around the object that matters**, not the account (3b0611c0). The title is a promise, not the name of the tab.
- **Progress is a climb, not a score**: where you are, what is left, the next level shown washed out so one wants to colour it (aeef9f7e). Seniority and streak at the top make a proof of progress (228c0e74).
- **Six doors explained rather than twenty rows.** Each sentence answers "what is this?" (7a42d03d). Sign-out is put away.
- **Completion reads on the avatar and the next question is already asked** (1dc9d964). One surface for personalisation, the rest stays neutral (a7d12283).

## Settings

- **Show the effect in place**: themes as tiles, a font in a real sentence (575182e9, 7b99e461). The first option follows the system, so nothing must be decided.
- **The value comes before its label** when people come to check it (e2fb2d59). A row can give a real state, « 2 active », so nothing needs opening (c0d73034).
- **The subject is what the person runs, not their account** (7c687c02). State first, then the two repair gestures.
- **Remind what the product has brought**, just above the subscription (834926b5). One promoted row only, with its picture returning on the next screen (bb1d5a9e).
- **The chevron announces only what opens** (a6b5300f). One colour on titles and icons signs a native screen.
- **A setting can be a gift**: names give each icon a personality (53d61058).

## Onboarding

- **The subject becomes the world of the first screen**; benefit lists leave it (b15ae3da). A split gives the text a calm ground below a coloured world (f4068c81).
- **Try before you are asked to stay**: a real micro-result before the commitment (1aab74cb). A real result as an example rather than a promise.
- **The promise has a date and a visible effect** (43d7747f). A first gesture wakes the character (c3edfbff).
- **The final button answers the question**: « That's me », never « Continue » (8d33bdf8). A button can announce what follows (0e9d8acc).
- **Each question gets its own control**, so twelve questions stay alive (b2ced6b9). A reassurance under a choice answers that exact objection.
- **Connection methods step back one notch** (4223ed1e).
- A test path, not a proof: get started, onboarding, commitment, loading, results, paywall, home, settings.

## Home

- **Answer one question first**: what today asks, what is left (aa04aea3, f282002f).
- **One editorial choice can replace a list**: a single item of the day, playable in place, makes a poster (e3fa37b1).
- **The stack shows the next item and, by its tab, how many follow**: two facts in one form (28539e2f).
- **Progress in the world** does the work of a bar (aec61475). Figures on top for who wants them, one written sentence below saying what they mean (6f791d4e).
- **Where people come to find themselves, search comes first** (9bb792fc).
- **Background work reads at a glance**: spinner and check, each job with its own face (ef1b61e8).

## Empty, loading, confirmation

- **An empty state sells the destination.** While there is nothing to navigate, remove the navigation and leave one action (3ea486db).
- **Waiting becomes a scene**, not a spinner (19cb9b98). The character shows what happens, one sentence says what comes next.
- **Show the object you are creating before naming it**, without a form (3517a55b).
- **The sheet takes the colours of what it covers**: you do not leave the object to adjust it (db7aac2c).
- **The button repeats the choice** (7865e1df) or counts what it will do: « Apply (5) » (ef6123d0).
- **Before generating, say what the AI will read and let a piece be removed** (83580129). A claim can trace to its evidence and source, each in its colour (030efd73).
- **Ask for a review as a person**: a signed letter with a face and a story (01dbebc9), and only after real value.
